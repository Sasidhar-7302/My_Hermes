# Enterprise Patterns

Use these patterns for larger product and enterprise designs in Paper.

## Core screens

- Dashboard: nav shell, KPI row, trend/chart area, table or queue, recent activity.
- CRUD workspace: nav shell, filter bar, record table/list, detail or side panel, create/edit form.
- Settings: category sidebar, section header, grouped forms, audit trail or history, permissions surface.
- Auth: clear hero or trust cue, single primary form, secondary help/footnote, realistic input labels.

## Visual defaults

- Prefer 3 levels of hierarchy: page title, section title, body/meta.
- Keep spacing asymmetric but controlled. Large outer padding, tighter inner groups.
- Avoid decorative gradients unless they clarify emphasis.
- Enterprise screens should show dense information without turning into cramped cards.

## Critique checklist

- Can a new user find the primary task in under 3 seconds?
- Does the data-dense area still have readable grouping?
- Are forms aligned and label/value relationships obvious?
- Are destructive or high-risk actions visually subordinate to primary flow?
- Is there enough state coverage for loading, empty, error, or audit/history surfaces?
